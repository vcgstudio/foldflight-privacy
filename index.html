Privacy Policy

App: FoldFlight: Garden Guardians (Android), including its Live Wallpaper

Developer: VCGStudio ("we", "us")

Privacy contact: vbcdgaming@gmail.com

Effective date: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information is accessed, collected, used and shared when you play FoldFlight: Garden Guardians ("FoldFlight", "the game") on Android, who receives it, how long it is kept and the choices you have.

1. Summary

  • There are no accounts. You do not sign in, and we do not ask for your name, email address or other contact details.
  • Your game progress and settings are saved on your device. We do not run servers, and the game does not send your personal information to us.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage information to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • The one optional purchase, Remove Ads, is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • The Unity game engine may send technical crash information to Unity.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

FoldFlight keeps the following on your device:

  • Game progress: missions completed and their stars, scores, coins, unlocked and selected planes, skins, weapons and boosters, and upgrade levels.
  • Settings: sound and music volumes, controls, graphics and similar preferences.
  • Live Wallpaper choices: the scenes and planes you picked and set, and the chapters and planes you have unlocked, so the wallpaper can show them without the game running.
  • Privacy notice record: the version of the in-game privacy notice you reviewed and when. This is not advertising consent.
  • Advertising consent choices, where you are asked for them, saved by Google's consent tool (see section 3).

Our code does not send this information off your device. Android keeps it in the game's private storage, which other apps cannot read. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this game data in your device backup, which Google stores for your Google account; you control this in your device's backup settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

FoldFlight shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one, for example to revive, to collect a bonus or to set the Live Wallpaper. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When the ad service starts and when ads are requested or shown, Google automatically collects and processes:

  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device;
  • App interactions: for example app launches, taps and ad views;
  • Diagnostic information: for example app launch time, hang rate and energy use of the app and the ads SDK.

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention: to deliver and measure ads and, where you allow it, to personalize them. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit. Google may work with its own advertising partners to fill ad requests. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Consent: the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). On each launch it checks with Google whether a consent message applies to you. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), you are asked for your choices, ads are requested only when Google's consent tool reports that they may be, and you can change your choices at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Android settings.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK adds these Android permissions: AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play)

The one purchase in the game, Remove Ads, is processed by Google Play Billing under Google's terms and privacy policy. The game asks Google Play whether it belongs to your Google account so it can unlock it, and checks Google's signature on the purchase record on your device. The Live Wallpaper is not sold. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. Google gives us order information (such as the order number, the item purchased and the purchase date) in the Google Play Console, which we use only for accounting, refunds and support.

5. Technical diagnostics (Unity)

FoldFlight is made with the Unity engine. The engine's diagnostics feature may send Unity technical crash and error information, such as error details and basic device and operating system information, so that Unity can diagnose and fix engine problems. Unity handles this information under its own privacy policy (https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).

The game does not include any other analytics, crash-reporting or tracking service.

6. Other features

  • Sharing: if you choose to share a result picture, Android's share sheet sends it to the app you pick. We do not receive it.
  • Live Wallpaper: you set it in the game by watching a rewarded ad (section 3), or with coins if you bought Remove Ads. The wallpaper itself runs on your device, uses only the information described in section 2, does not show ads and does not connect to the internet.
  • Permissions: besides the advertising permissions above, the game uses INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (ads and purchases) and BILLING (Google Play purchases). WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE are added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the Android libraries it uses. The game does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

7. How information is used, and legal bases

We use the information described here only to run the game, to show the ads you choose to watch and to provide your purchases. Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies:

  • Personalized advertising and the use of device identifiers for it: your consent, collected through Google's consent tool. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Non-personalized ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention: our and Google's legitimate interests in funding a free game.
  • Purchases: performance of a contract with you.
  • Technical diagnostics: legitimate interests in keeping the game working.

8. Who receives information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it ourselves. The services below receive information directly from your device under their own privacy policies:

  • Google LLC: AdMob ads, the User Messaging Platform and Google Play Billing (and Android backup, if you use it).
  • Unity Technologies: engine crash and error diagnostics.

9. Security

The game stores its data in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot access. Google's and Unity's services receive data over encrypted connections. Purchases are unlocked only after Google's signature on the purchase record has been checked. We do not operate servers that hold your data.

10. Retention and deletion

Data stored by the game stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings (Settings > Apps > FoldFlight: Garden Guardians > Storage > Clear storage); either one deletes it. Data in an Android backup is kept and deleted under your Google account's backup settings. Information received by Google or Unity is kept according to their own retention policies. Google Play keeps your purchase history with your Google account; purchases are restored when you reinstall the game. Order records we receive from Google Play are kept as long as needed for accounting, tax and refund obligations.

There are no accounts in FoldFlight, so there is no account to delete.

11. Your rights and choices

  • Change or withdraw advertising consent in Settings > Privacy choices (shown where it applies to you).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Android settings.
  • Delete all game data by clearing the game's storage or uninstalling it.
  • Read this policy at any time in Settings > Privacy Policy.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Californian law), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we do not hold your personal information on our own systems, requests about advertising or diagnostic data are best directed to Google or Unity, but we will help with any request sent to the contact address below.

12. Children

FoldFlight is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help to have it removed.

13. International processing

Google and Unity may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, under the safeguards described in their privacy policies.

14. This website

This policy is published on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. The page has no cookies, analytics, ads, forms or scripts. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement (https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). This applies to the website only and is separate from the game's data described above.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. We will update the effective date above, and when a change matters for you, the game will show its privacy notice again before you continue.

16. Contact
